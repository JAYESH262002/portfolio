METHODOLOGY / THE 4-STAGE OPERATING LOOP

How Problems Turn Into Scalable Execution

Operations fail when organizations leap from a high-level symptom to a software purchase without understanding the human and physical handoffs. Here is the 4-stage loop I apply.

01

Stage 01: Direct Physical Observation (Walk the Floor)

Map the journey of a physical carton, invoice, or purchase order from receipt to dispatch. Identify where physical friction, waiting time, or undocumented workarounds occur.

Real Application: Auditing Mumbai retailer ordering patterns at the counter to spot why stockouts occurred despite distributor inventory.
02

Stage 02: Unit Economic & Mathematical Modeling

Convert physical observations into an accurate mathematical model. Calculate true landed costs, safety stock buffers, reorder points, and holding cost sensitivity before proposing changes.

Real Application: Building Only Solar's multi-tier landed-cost formulas factoring shipping line bunker fees and customs tariffs.
03

Stage 03: Scalable Relational Systems & SOPs

Codify the solution into durable relational database schemas, clear standard operating procedures, and automated alerts so execution does not depend on heroics or memory.

Real Application: Structuring SQL tables with automated stockout triggers and Power BI fulfillment dashboards.
04

Stage 04: The Closed-Loop Feedback Mechanism

Every delivery, return, and customer interaction creates telemetry. Feed frontline delivery performance and retail sell-through directly back into upstream procurement schedules.

Real Application: CavinKare weekly beat reviews matching primary factory dispatches to secondary counter sales velocity.
