METHODOLOGY / THE 4-STAGE OPERATING LOOP

How Problems Turn Into Scalable Execution

Operations succeed when organizations align frontline physical reality with disciplined commercial economics and reliable systems. Here is the 4-stage loop I apply.

01

Stage 01: Direct Physical Observation (Walk the Floor)

Map the journey of a physical carton, order booking, or supplier shipment from source to shelf. Identify where physical friction, waiting time, or undocumented bottlenecks occur.

Real Application: Auditing Mumbai retail ordering patterns at the counter to spot why stockouts occurred despite distributor warehouse inventory.
02

Stage 02: Unit Economic & Cost Modeling

Convert physical observations into an accurate cost model. Calculate true landed costs, freight allocations, customs tariffs, and buffer inventory before committing capital.

Real Application: Building landed-cost models factoring shipping lines, port clearance tariffs, and domestic linehaul for lighting imports at Only Solar.
03

Stage 03: Scalable Relational Systems & SOPs

Codify the solution into durable relational database schemas, standard operating procedures, and automated alerts so execution does not depend on memory or manual spreadsheets.

Real Application: Structuring relational order-tracking schemas and replenishment alert specifications based on Computer Science engineering fundamentals.
04

Stage 04: The Closed-Loop Feedback Mechanism

Every delivery, return, and retailer interaction creates telemetry. Feed frontline delivery performance and retail sell-through directly back into upstream procurement schedules.

Real Application: Regular CavinKare beat reviews matching order bookings directly against distributor closing stock to protect on-shelf availability.
